# Code of Conduct

We want superv to be a welcoming project for everyone.

## Our standards

- Be respectful and constructive. Assume good faith.
- Welcome newcomers and be patient with questions.
- Give and accept feedback on work, not on people.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, trolling, or publishing others' private information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, and contributions that violate this code, and may ban contributors for repeated or serious violations.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through GitHub (a private message or a security advisory draft addressed to the maintainers). Reports are handled confidentially.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
