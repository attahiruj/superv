All notable changes are documented here. Format based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- `.deb` packaging (`cargo deb -p superv`) with autostart, D-Bus activation, icon and AppStream metadata
- First-run setup of the GNOME Shell extension (installed and enabled by the app itself)
- Draft Snap and Flatpak manifests
- Clipboard history for text and images with pinning and search
- Emoji and symbol picker
- X11, wlroots/KDE Wayland and GNOME Wayland (Shell extension) capture
- TOML configuration, size filtering; password-manager copies are recorded
